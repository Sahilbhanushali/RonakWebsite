Frequently Asked Questions

What documents are required for a home loan application?

Typical documents include:

  • Identity proof (PAN Card, Aadhaar, Passport)
  • Address proof (Utility bills, Rental agreement)
  • Income proof (Salary slips, Bank statements)
  • Property documents (Sale agreement, Title deeds)
  • Photographs

How long does loan approval take?

Approval timelines vary by loan type:

  • Home loans: 3-7 working days after document submission
  • Loan against property: 5-10 working days
  • Personal loans: 1-3 working days
